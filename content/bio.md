I am a second-year CS PhD student at the [School of Computing and Augmented Intelligence, Arizona State University](https://scai.engineering.asu.edu/), advised by [Prof. Hua Wei](https://labs.engineering.asu.edu/hw/) in the DaRL Lab. I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from [Xi'an Jiaotong University](http://en.xjtu.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Yuanqi Su](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hXQYvl8AAAAJ).

I aim to understand how large language models can learn from experience and improve themselves as efficiently and effectively as humans do with a perspective in neuroscience and cognitive science. This question guides my research on memory design, LLM architectures, and the learning signals that drive adaptation. I am also interested in building intelligent agent systems for real-world applications, including autonomous driving, transportation, and education.
